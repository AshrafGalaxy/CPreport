## 3.2 Soft Computing Methodologies
The system leverages soft computing methodologies:
- **Ensemble Regression:** Captures capacity degradation trends across operational cycles.
- **Mamdani Fuzzy Inference:** Translates margin and state variables into continuous operational urgencies.
- **Pareto Evolutionary Optimization:** Resolves multi-objective trade-offs between deficit coverage and capital costs.
