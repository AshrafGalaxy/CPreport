# CHAPTER 1: INTRODUCTION

## 1.1 Context and Motivation
Modern cyber-physical systems demand tight integration between computational models and real-world physical constraints. In distributed environments and energy networks, computational decisions directly influence hardware reliability, lifetime degradation, and operational costs.

## 1.2 Problem Statement
Traditional systems treat dynamic components as static black boxes with nominal factory ratings. Under compound thermal and payload stresses, these static assumptions fail catastrophically, creating reliability bottlenecks and financial penalties.

## 1.3 Project Objectives
The objective of this work is to formulate, implement, and benchmark an automated intelligence framework capable of:
1. Modeling real-world physical degradation in real-time.
2. Formulating intelligent dispatch and decision policies under operational uncertainty.
3. Optimizing resource infrastructure placement using multi-objective evolutionary search.
