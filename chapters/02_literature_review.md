# CHAPTER 2: LITERATURE REVIEW

## 2.1 State of the Art
A comprehensive review of existing literature reveals that contemporary approaches are divided into purely data-driven machine learning models and classical mathematical programming heuristics.

## 2.2 Research Gaps
1. **Absence of Unified Feedback:** Most existing platforms decouple operational dispatch from long-term infrastructure planning.
2. **Computational Overhead:** Physics-heavy simulations often incur latencies unsuitable for real-time edge gateways.
